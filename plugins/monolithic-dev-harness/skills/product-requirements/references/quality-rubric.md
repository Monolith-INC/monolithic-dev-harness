# PRD Quality Rubric

Read the full PRD and addendum before judging it. Calibrate rigor to the stated stakes and product
shape. Findings cite a precise section, state impact, and propose a fix.

For each dimension, judge `strong`, `adequate`, `thin`, or `broken`:

1. **Decision readiness** — decisions and trade-offs are explicit; open questions are genuinely
   open; objections are acknowledged rather than smoothed away.
2. **Substance over theater** — personas, differentiation, vision, and NFRs each change a decision;
   boilerplate furniture is removed.
3. **Strategic coherence** — features and priorities serve one thesis; success metrics test that
   thesis rather than merely measuring activity.
4. **Done clarity** — each `FR-N` has concrete, verifiable consequences; quality requirements use
   bounds rather than adjectives.
5. **Scope honesty** — non-goals, assumptions, and deferrals are visible; open-item density is safe
   for the next phase.
6. **Downstream usability** — terminology is stable; `FR-N`, `UJ-N`, and `SM-N` identifiers are
   unique; cross-references resolve; extracted sections still make sense.
7. **Shape fit** — user journeys are used when experience is load-bearing and omitted when they are
   theater; regulation, brownfield constraints, and technical-product contracts get the rigor they
   actually require.

Conclude with a short overall verdict, critical/high findings first, and a compact tail for
medium/low mechanical issues such as ID gaps, glossary drift, or unindexed assumptions.
