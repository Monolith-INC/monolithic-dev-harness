# Product Brief Shape

Adapt this shape to the purpose. Omit sections that add no decision value, add domain-specific
sections when needed, and keep the main brief to roughly one or two pages.

```markdown
---
title: <product or initiative>
status: draft | final
created: YYYY-MM-DD
updated: YYYY-MM-DD
sources: []
---

# Product Brief: <name>

## Executive Summary

<What this is, the problem it solves, why it matters, and why now.>

## Problem

<Who feels the pain, how they cope today, and the cost of the status quo.>

## Proposed Outcome

<The experience and outcome, without prescribing implementation.>

## Who This Serves

<Primary users and what success means for them; important non-users when useful.>

## Why This Approach

<Honest differentiation and rejected alternatives that affect the decision.>

## Success Criteria

<Observable user and business outcomes.>

## First-Version Scope

### In

- <boundary>

### Out

- <boundary and reason when it matters>

## Longer-Term Direction

<Only when it changes a near-term decision.>

## Assumptions and Open Questions

- <explicit item>
```

Put technical mechanisms, detailed personas, sizing, option matrices, and parked roadmap depth in
`addendum.md` when they are load-bearing but would make the brief stop being brief.
