# Agent instructions

Superpowers skills write their artifacts under `.superpowers/`, never under `docs/`: specs to
`.superpowers/specs/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>-design.md` and plans to
`.superpowers/plans/YYYY-MM-DD-<feature-name>.md`. The folder is ignored by git. A document that
outlives the work belongs to its work item under `docs/work-items/`, named as Article 8a of the
[constitution](CONSTITUTION.md) requires.
